OLC Planner

Add to your home screen

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen. Leave Open as Web App on.
  3. Tap Add, then open the planner from the bear icon.

Add to your home screen

  1. Tap the menu in Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install.
  3. Open the planner from the new icon.

The icon opens the planner full screen. The first time, it may ask you to sign in to Google.
